# InviteMe template collection assets

Created on 2026-10-13. Reference catalog order: https://chungdoi.com/vi/mau-thiep

The references were inspected for composition, shapes and color families. No photograph or decoration was downloaded from Chung Đôi for this collection. Existing copied assets in `minimalism-brown` remain on disk for compatibility, but none of the 15 catalog documents references them. Brown node coordinates were preserved while its references were replaced. Burgundy retains its previously generated fictional couple and original SVG decoration.

## Built-in imagegen outputs

All people are fictional adults. Images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then resized and encoded as WebP using Sharp. Transparent illustrations preserve alpha. Generated originals remain in Codex's generated-images directory; project assets are the WebP files beside this document.

## Code-native SVG assets

`envelope-green.svg`, `envelope-brown.svg`, `wave.svg`, `olive-arch.svg`, `silk.svg`, `paper.svg`, `polaroid.svg`, `gate.svg`, `cake.svg`, and `water.svg` were authored specifically for InviteMe. They contain no external image, script or source-site artwork.

## Intentional differences

The templates are editable recreations of the referenced compositions, with original art and fictional demo content. They are not pixel-identical copies. Typography uses the locally installed EB Garamond. Gallery presentation, calendar, maps, RSVP, gift dialog and wishes use InviteMe's shared widgets; demo submissions remain local and are not connected to .NET. No copyrighted background music is included.
