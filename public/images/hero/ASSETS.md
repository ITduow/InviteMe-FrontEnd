# Hero raster artwork

Only assets referenced by the current hero are kept in this directory.

- `wedding-poster.webp`: still frame derived from the Pexels video. Source and license are recorded in `../../videos/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
- `botanical-stem-v2.webp`, `botanical-leaf.webp`, `botanical-flower.webp`, `botanical-gold.webp`: original AI-generated transparent watercolor parts. Separate pale sage stems/leaves, ivory flowers and fine gold twigs let the opening branches reveal individual leaves progressively.
- `reference-botanical-v1.webp`: built-in ImageGen reconstruction using the supplied Joy screenshot as reference. Generated silver sage eucalyptus, green seedpods and fine gold outline branches with a transparent background; UI, photo, card and text were removed. This is a generated reconstruction, not the original Joy PNG.

Superseded watercolor branches, sprig sheets and willow experiments were removed from the served asset directory during cleanup on 2026-10-08.
